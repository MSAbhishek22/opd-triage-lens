03 — The dial

      
One disc. Four colours.
Every rule lives on it.

      
Move your pointer over the dial — it tilts in 3D. The needle points at the colour of your last verdict. Tap a quadrant to filter the rule book below.

      

        

          

            
            
            
            
            
OPD

            

          

        

        

          

            
RED
Emergency — go now, call 108. Chest pain with breathlessness, unconsciousness, stroke signs, heavy bleeding.

            
AMBER
Urgent — same-day OPD slot. Chest pain alone, high fever, severe stomach pain, repeated vomiting.

            
GREEN
Routine — next available slot. Mild fever, cold and cough, headache, rash, back pain.

            
BLUE
Self-care — no urgent slot. Checkups, vaccines, tiredness, small cuts, diet questions.

          

          
The needle only moves when a real verdict exists. An honest “cannot triage” leaves it at rest — the dial never pretends to know.

05 — How it works

      
Deterministic. Auditable.
Nothing to hide.

      
    
        
  1. 1 · Match. Your words are matched against 34 written rules (English + Hinglish keywords). Phrases count double; single words count once.
    2. 
        
  2. 2 · Second look. Four combination rules run — chest pain + breathlessness is an emergency even though chest pain alone is not. This is the part a keyword list alone gets wrong.
    3. 
        
  3. 3 · Modifiers. Duration (fever 3+ days), age group (child with fever) and vitals (SpO2 under 92%) adjust the verdict — each by an explicit, listed rule.
    4. 
        
  4. 4 · Handoff. The verdict, the matched rule, the confidence band and the slot advice are written into a structured summary for the doctor — with the refusal path kept open.
    5. 
      

      

        

          
Measured, not claimed

          
—

          
The number is agreement between the engine and our own labels — a correctness check on the rule book, not a claim about real patients. The labelled set is published in corpus.js; anyone can re-run it with node tests/test.js.

        

        

          
What it does NOT do

          
    
            
  • No diagnosis, no prescription, no treatment advice.
    • 
            
  • No model, no API call, no data leaves your device — everything runs in this page.
    • 
            
  • Unknown symptoms get an honest “cannot triage” — never a guessed colour.
    • 
            
  • It does not replace a clinician. Red flags always point to emergency care first.
    • 
          

        

        

          
Why it fits Future Health

          
Statement #4 — Precision Prevention and Screening: it identifies people with unmet or elevated risk and connects them to the right pathway at the right time, in Hinglish as well as English — personalized and culturally appropriate.

          
Statement #5 — Closing the Screening-to-Care Loop: every actionable result becomes a structured, documented handoff with clear ownership (the patient carries it to the clinician) and a closed loop — triage → slot → handoff.
