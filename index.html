RED
Emergency — go now, call 108. Chest pain with breathlessness, unconsciousness, stroke signs, heavy bleeding.

            
AMBER
Urgent — same-day OPD slot. Chest pain alone, high fever, severe stomach pain, repeated vomiting.

            
GREEN
Routine — next available slot. Mild fever, cold and cough, headache, rash, back pain.

            
BLUE
Self-care — no urgent slot. Checkups, vaccines, tiredness, small cuts, diet questions.

          

          
The needle only moves when a real verdict exists. An honest “cannot triage” leaves it at rest — the dial never pretends to know.

Before you start: consent

        
This check runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent to a server, stored online, or shared with anyone. There is no account and no backend.

        
Important boundary: this is a screening aid only. It is not a diagnosis, it does not identify any disease or condition, and it must not be used as medical advice.

        
Who this is for: adults aged 18 and over. It does not assess children — for a child, the right next step is a Well-Child Visit; ask a clinician about it.

    Documentation — for clinicians
    
        
    Assumptions
    
        
      
          
    • All inputs are self-reported and may be incomplete or approximate; unknown values add no points and are shown as “not provided”.
      • 
          
    • Scores are transparent, rule-based sums of the listed factors — not a statistical model, not trained on patient data, and not validated clinically.
      • 
          
    • Thresholds are deliberately simple: under 35 = Lower, 35–64 = Review, 65 and over = Strong. They are triage prompts, not clinical cut-offs.
      • 
          
    • Family history is asked only in broad categories (diabetes / heart disease in parents or siblings) — never in detail.
      • 
          
    • This is a local, single-page prototype with no server, no account, and no data storage beyond this browser session.
      • 
        
    
        
    Limitations
    
        
      
          
    • This is screening only, not a diagnosis, and not medical advice. It cannot rule anything in or out.
      • 
          
    • It does not consider medicines, pregnancy, or any condition you may already have — tell a clinician about those directly.
      • 
          
    • It identifies signals for one person at a time; it does not combine population or community signals, and it does not assess children — for a child, the right next step is a Well-Child Visit (see the consent screen).
      • 
          
    • It has not been reviewed or endorsed by any clinician, clinic, or health authority. No partnership or endorsement is claimed.
      • 
          
    • Results depend entirely on what you entered; different inputs give different signals, and that is expected.
      • 
        
    
        
    Data-use statement
    
        
    Your inputs stay in this browser. Nothing is sent to a server, no account is created, and nothing is stored online. Closing this page discards the inputs. If a future version ever added a server, that change would need a new consent screen first.
    
        
    
          
          
        
    
      
    
    
    
  
    

  
  
    
    
    
      
    05 — How it works
    
      
    Deterministic. Auditable.
    Nothing to hide.
    
      
      
        
    1. 1 · Match. Your words are matched against 34 written rules (English + Hinglish keywords). Phrases count double; single words count once.
      2. 
        
    2. 2 · Second look. Four combination rules run — chest pain + breathlessness is an emergency even though chest pain alone is not. This is the part a keyword list alone gets wrong.
      3. 
        
    3. 3 · Modifiers. Duration (fever 3+ days), age group (child with fever) and vitals (SpO2 under 92%) adjust the verdict — each by an explicit, listed rule.
      4. 
        
    4. 4 · Handoff. The verdict, the matched rule, the confidence band and the slot advice are written into a structured summary for the doctor — with the refusal path kept open.
      5. 
      
    
      
    How Prevent works
    
      
      
        
    1. 1 · Consent. Two checkboxes — screening-only, local-only — before any input is entered. Continue stays disabled until both are checked.
      2. 
        
    2. 2 · Inputs. Age band, family history in broad categories, lifestyle factors, and markers you already know. Every field except consent is optional; “Not known” is a first-class answer.
      3. 
        
    3. 3 · Signals. Three weighted scores (metabolic, cardiovascular, sleep/stress), each with the exact inputs that moved it listed underneath. The urgent screen comes only from the “worrying symptoms right now” checkbox — never from a score.
      4. 
        
    4. 4 · Schedule. A dated list of preventive checks — annual, 2-yearly or 5-yearly by risk band — with referral pathways and a check-off list, plus a prevention plan you can carry to a clinician.
      5. 
      
    
      
    
        
    
          
    Measured, not claimed
    
          
    —
    
          
    The number is agreement between the engine and our own labels — a correctness check on the rule book, not a claim about real patients. The labelled set is published in corpus.js; anyone can re-run it with node tests/test.js.
    
        
    
        
    
          
    What it does NOT do
    
          
      
            
    • No diagnosis, no prescription, no treatment advice.
      • 
            
    • No model, no API call, no data leaves your device — everything runs in this page.
      • 
            
    • Unknown symptoms get an honest “cannot triage” — never a guessed colour.
      • 
            
    • It does not replace a clinician. Red flags always point to emergency care first.
      • 
          
    
        
    
        
    
          
    Why it fits Future Health
    
          
    Statement #4 — Precision Prevention and Screening: it identifies people with unmet or elevated risk and connects them to the right pathway at the right time, in Hinglish as well as English — personalized and culturally appropriate.
    
          
    Statement #5 — Closing the Screening-to-Care Loop: every actionable result becomes a structured, documented handoff with clear ownership (the patient carries it to the clinician) and a closed loop — triage → slot → handoff.
